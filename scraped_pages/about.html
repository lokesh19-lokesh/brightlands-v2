    Who We Are?

    BrightLands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic

    BrightLands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic is much more than a skin clinic and medical treatment location. We have proudly placed our clinic in exceptional locations to make it easily accessible to all.

    Our clinic is headed by Chief Consultant Dermatologist Dr. Sania Riaz, (MBBS, MD DVL), Consultant Dermatologist, Cosmetologist & Trichologist. Dr. Sania is excessively passionate about delivering solutions that are result-focused and provide utmost client satisfaction. She has received multiple awards for her excellence in the field of Dermatology.

    We, at the clinic, are dedicated to your health, ensuring your safety and beautifying you in all ways from within and above!

    We are specifically skilled with a devoted and specialist team, however we are backed up by our excellent state-of-the-art, technology. Our constantly updated state-of-the-art equipment and machinery, be it, the US FDA APPROVED Laser, the HIFU machines or our Cosmetic Gynecology laser machines, all are advanced, modern outcome-driven machines- just like us!!

    Mission

    To deliver expert and affordable skin and hair care while promoting awareness and well-being in our community.

    Vision

    To be a trusted center of excellence, offering comprehensive solutions for skin, hair, and cosmetic concerns.

    Values

    We value compassion, honesty, empathy, and patient-focused care, ensuring holistic and ethical treatments always.
