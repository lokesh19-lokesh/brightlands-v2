Clinical Dermatology

Comprehensive Medical Skin Care

Clinical dermatology focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and treating pathological skin, hair, and nail conditions. Skin is your body’s largest organ, and symptoms like rashes, persistent breakouts, or lesions frequently point to underlying hormonal, genetic, immunological, or lifestyle factors.

At Brightlands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic, our consultants avoid cookie-cutter prescriptions. Every patient undergoes high-resolution dermoscopic examination and comprehensive medical history review to identify the precise root cause before formulating a personalized regimen.

Conditions We Frequently Treat

Severe Acne & Rosacea

Cystic acne, hormonal comedones, redness, and inflammation treated with topical and oral therapies.

Eczema & Dermatitis

Atopic and contact dermatitis relief, skin barrier repair, and allergy trigger avoidance.

Psoriasis & Scalp Flares

Chronic plaque psoriasis, inverse psoriasis, and scalp buildup targeted with evidence-based management.

Fungal & Bacterial Infections

Tinea infections, folliculitis, candidiasis, and stubborn recurrent skin lesions cured with lab diagnostics.

Our 4-Step Consultation Process

1
Detailed Clinical History & Exam

Review of diet, medication, environmental triggers, and genetic predispositions.

2
Dermoscopic Diagnostic Analysis

Microscopic examination of lesion margins, vascular patterns, and follicular health.

3
Customized Treatment Protocol

Prescription medicines, medical-grade skin barrier restoration, and in-clinic procedures.

4
Monitored Follow-Up & Maintenance

Periodic evaluation to assess clinical recovery and transition to preventative skin health.
