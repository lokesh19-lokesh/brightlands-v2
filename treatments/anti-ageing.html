Anti-Ageing Treatments

Subtle, elegant rejuvenation treatments that soften dynamic wrinkles, restore lost volume, and lift sagging contours while preserving your authentic expressions.

Ethical Aesthetic Medicine

As skin matures, intrinsic biological aging reduces hyaluronic acid, elastin, and collagen, while repetitive muscle contractions form dynamic wrinkles on the forehead, crow's feet, and frown lines. At Bright Lands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic, our medical specialists prioritize subtle, balanced enhancements rather than "frozen" or overfilled faces.

Procedures Performed

Botox & Wrinkle Relaxers

USFDA-approved neurotoxin precisely micro-injected to soften crow's feet, forehead lines, and frown lines.

Dermal Fillers

Cross-linked hyaluronic acid gels that restore cheek volume, define jawlines, and soften nasolabial folds.

Skin Boosters & Profhilo

Ultra-pure bio-remodeling hyaluronic acid that stimulates natural collagen remodeling for crepey skin.

RF Skin Tightening

Non-invasive radiofrequency energy that heats dermal layers to contract collagen and tighten loose jawlines.

Frequently Asked Questions

At Bright Lands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic, our doctors practice the "micro-dose" and "baby Botox" technique. We selectively relax only the hyperactive muscle bands causing deep creases, preserving your natural eyebrow movement and genuine smiles.
Botox results generally last 4 to 6 months as nerve receptors naturally regenerate. Hyaluronic acid dermal fillers typically last between 9 and 18 months depending on the area treated and individual metabolic rate.
