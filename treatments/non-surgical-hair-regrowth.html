Cellular Awakening for Thinning Hair

When hair follicles undergo miniaturization from genetics (androgenetic alopecia), nutritional stress, or hormonal imbalance, non-surgical cellular therapy can halt the cycle and reactivate thicker anagen growth. At Bright Lands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic, our trichology clinic uses advanced closed-system centrifugation to harvest pure growth factors with zero red blood cell contamination.

Proven Non-Surgical Protocols

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC)

Next-generation acellular therapy delivering PDGF, VEGF, EGF, and IGF-1 directly from your activated platelets without pain.

High-Concentration PRP Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma micro-injections that increase scalp microcirculation and prolong the active hair growth phase.

QR678 Neo Biomimetic Therapy

Patented synthetic bio-peptide cocktail micro-infused into the scalp, clinically proven to stop genetic pattern hair loss.

Medical Trichology Regimens

Doctor-prescribed topical minoxidil formulations, peptide foams, anti-androgen therapies, and micronutrient supplementation.
