The Difference of a Dermatologist-Formulated Medi Facial

Unlike salon facials that use generic creams, abrasive scrubs, or harsh steam, Bright Lands Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic Medi Facials are clinical-grade treatments curated by doctors. We analyze your skin sebum index, hydration levels, and sensitivity threshold before personalizing the serums.

Key Medi Facial Steps

1. Vortex Cleansing

Gentle suction removes dead stratum corneum cells and sebum blockage without manual pinching.

2. Gentle Acid Peel Infusion

Glycolic and salicylic acid solution loosens stubborn blackheads and comedones in the T-zone.

3. Painless Extraction

Automated vacuum suction draws out trapped impurities and environmental pollution debris.

4. Antioxidant & Peptide Hydration

Hyaluronic acid, vitamins, and brightening peptides deeply saturate the skin for a plump, luminous finish.
