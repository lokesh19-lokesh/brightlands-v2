Dr. Surya Keerthana

MBBS, MS, MCh (Plastic Surgery)

Senior Consultant Plastic, Cosmetic and Reconstructive Surgeon

Dr. Surya Keerthana is a highly accomplished Plastic, Cosmetic and Reconstructive Surgeon with super-specialty training in modern hair restoration surgery, precision dermato-surgery, and cosmetic gynecology.

With super-specialized MCh credentials in plastic surgery, Dr. Keerthana brings surgical precision to hair transplantation (FUE), complex scar revision, and aesthetic body contouring. Her compassionate approach prioritizes patient safety, natural aesthetic symmetry, and minimal recovery times.
