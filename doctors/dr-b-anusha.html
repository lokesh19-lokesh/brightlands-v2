Dr. B. Anusha

MBBS, DDVL

Consultant Dermatologist, Cosmetologist, Trichologist

Dr. B. Anusha brings dedicated clinical dermatology, trichology, and aesthetic skincare expertise, passionate about delivering gentle, effective dermatological solutions for skin barrier repair.

Her clinical practice encompasses medical management of refractory adolescent and adult acne, superficial chemical peel treatments, chronic dermatitis, fungal infections, and laser procedures. Known for her approachable bedside manner, Dr. Anusha empowers patients through proper skincare routine education.
