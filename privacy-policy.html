Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Patient Confidentiality

At Brightlands Derma (“Brightlands”, “we”, “us”), we take your privacy and the protection of your personal health data seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal, medical, and contact information collected through our website (brightlandsderma.com) and our physical clinic operations in Gachibowli and Toli Chowki, Hyderabad.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our appointment booking form or contact our clinic, we may collect:

  • Personal Identity: Full Name, gender, age.
  • Contact Details: Mobile number, email address, residential locality.
  • Appointment Preferences: Preferred clinic location (Gachibowli or Toli Chowki), selected doctor, treatment requested, and preferred appointment time.
  • Clinical Inquiries: Voluntary descriptions of skin or hair concerns submitted via consultation request forms.

3. Use of Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Confirm, reschedule, and manage clinical appointments.
  • Enable our doctors to prepare pre-consultation medical profiles.
  • Communicate appointment reminders, pre-procedure preparatory instructions, and post-procedure care advice.
  • Comply with applicable healthcare regulations and National Medical Commission record retention guidelines.

4. Data Protection & Third Parties

Brightlands Derma does not sell, lease, or monetize patient contact lists or medical data to third-party commercial marketers. Information is disclosed only to authorized medical personnel, nursing staff, or secure IT infrastructure providers under non-disclosure obligations.
