How to Choose the Right Dermatologist for Your Skin Concerns

Dr. Sania Riaz (MBBS, MD, DVL)
Chief Consultant Dermatologist • Published October 2026 • 5 min read

Skin health is intimately connected with physical comfort and personal confidence. With an increasing number of aesthetic parlors marketing medical treatments, understanding how to select a genuinely qualified dermatologist has never been more vital.

1. Verify Official Medical Qualifications

In India, a recognized dermatologist must possess an MBBS degree followed by specialized postgraduate training: an MD in Dermatology, Venereology, and Leprosy (DVL) or a DDVL diploma from an accredited medical university recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC). Be cautious of titles like "Aesthetician" or "Cosmetic Expert" when seeking treatment for clinical conditions like acne, psoriasis, or unexplained rashes.

2. Look for Evidence-Based Diagnostic Standards

An experienced dermatologist will rarely prescribe aggressive medications or invasive procedures without performing a thorough physical examination, digital dermatoscopy, and reviewing your lifestyle, family medical history, and blood parameters. A reputable clinic will always emphasize root-cause healing over quick cosmetic cover-ups.

3. USFDA-Approved Equipment and Sterile Protocols

When considering laser hair removal, laser toning, chemical peels, or micro-needling, inquire about the machinery used. Devices cleared by the USFDA adhere to stringent energy output consistency and integrated skin cooling, dramatically reducing the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) on Indian skin types.

Key Takeaway from Dr. Sania Riaz:

“Never hesitate to ask your doctor about their postgraduate qualifications, expected procedural timelines, and realistic outcomes. Ethical dermatology is built entirely on clinical transparency.”

4. Clear, Ethical Pricing Without Unrealistic Guarantees

Biological skin cells regenerate across 28-day cycles, and genuine follicular hair cycles require multiple sessions. Steer clear of providers claiming "100% guaranteed permanent cure in 1 day". A trustworthy dermatology clinic will provide transparent session packages, realistic expectation timelines, and custom maintenance routines.
